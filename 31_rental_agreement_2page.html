RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made and executed at _____________ on this _____________ by and between:

__________________________, S/o, D/o, W/o ____________________, aged about ____ years, residing at ________________________________________________________________________.

Hereinafter called the "LESSOR" (Owner) of the FIRST PART.

AND

__________________________, S/o, D/o, W/o ____________________, aged about ____ years, residing at ________________________________________________________________________.

Hereinafter called the "LESSEE" (Tenant) of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the residential property situated at ________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________ and has agreed to let out the same to the Lessee for a period of 11 Months.

NOW THIS RENTAL AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS UNDER:

1.The Monthly Rent for the schedule premises is fixed at Rs. __________/- per month, payable on or before 5th of each month.
2.The Lessee has paid a sum of Rs. __________/- as interest-free Security Deposit to the Lessor, which is refundable at the time of vacating subject to deductions if any.
3.The period of this agreement shall be for 11 Months starting from the date of execution. This can be renewed with mutual consent with a 5% to 10% hike in rent.
4.The Lessee shall pay the electricity charges and water charges separately to the concerned departments as per the meter readings.
5.The Lessee shall use the premises only for residential purposes and shall not sub-let or assign the premises to any third party without prior written permission of the Lessor.
6.The Lessee shall not make any structural changes or permanent alterations to the premises without the prior written consent of the Lessor.
7.The Lessor or his authorized agent shall have the right to inspect the premises at reasonable times with prior notice to the Lessee.
8.Either party can terminate this agreement by giving ONE MONTH clear notice in writing or pay one month rent in lieu of such notice.
9.At the time of vacating the premises, the Lessee shall hand over the same in good condition as it was at the commencement of the lease, normal wear and tear excepted.
10.Any disputes arising out of this agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in _________ only.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this agreement on the day, month and year first above written.

LESSOR (OWNER)
WITNESSES:

1. ____________________________

2. ____________________________
LESSEE (TENANT)
